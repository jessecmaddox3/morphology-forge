# Teaching bank and references

> **TL;DR:** This is an original, AI-assisted bank of word-building prompts, hints and explanations. All original targets are retained. The public release adds missing practice routes and corrects several misleading explanations; it is not a validated curriculum.

Seventeen bases have both build and inference demonstrations. The full bank contains 62 build targets, 18 inferences and 14 relation cards. Separate build/happy/teach entries avoid giving one root credit for another. Thermal supplies a build route for thermo; revise, autobiography, photometer, builder, happily and reteach supply missing inference routes. Definitions and prompts are newly written rather than copied dictionary entries.

The distinction between word structure and word history matters. A historical family can be real even when a literal modern compound is not. The knowledge card therefore asks about its relationship to know, not whether the ordinary word ledge contributes meaning. Borrowed endings in audience, biography and symphony receive contextual explanations rather than a universal gloss. [Merriam-Webster: knowledge](https://www.merriam-webster.com/dictionary/knowledge), [audience](https://www.merriam-webster.com/dictionary/audience), [-y](https://www.merriam-webster.com/dictionary/-y), [-graphy](https://www.merriam-webster.com/dictionary/-graphy), [symphony](https://www.merriam-webster.com/dictionary/symphony).

The transcript chips use tran + script, teaching tran- as a form of trans- before s. There is no general rule that repeated letters in unrelated word sums disappear. The spelling note appears in feedback. [Collins: tran-](https://www.collinsdictionary.com/us/dictionary/english/tran), [Merriam-Webster: transcript](https://www.merriam-webster.com/dictionary/transcript), [transcribe](https://www.merriam-webster.com/dictionary/transcribe).

Other corrections preserve their original teaching purpose: porter replaces airport as an example of the carry root; un- includes reversing an action; circum- examples actually contain that prefix; bene- no longer carries an unsupported Spanish derivation; resign uses its documented history; geothermal distinguishes geo, therm and al. [Merriam-Webster: port](https://www.merriam-webster.com/dictionary/port), [Oxford University Press: un-](https://blog.oup.com/2021/01/understanding-un/), [circum-](https://www.merriam-webster.com/dictionary/circum-), [RAE: bueno](https://dle.rae.es/bueno), [resign](https://www.merriam-webster.com/dictionary/resign), [therm-/thermo-](https://www.merriam-webster.com/dictionary/therm).

The new word meanings were checked against dictionary entries for [revise](https://www.merriam-webster.com/dictionary/revise), [autobiography](https://www.merriam-webster.com/dictionary/autobiography), [photometer](https://www.merriam-webster.com/dictionary/photometer), [builder](https://www.merriam-webster.com/dictionary/builder), [reteach](https://www.merriam-webster.com/dictionary/reteach) and [thermal](https://www.merriam-webster.com/dictionary/thermal). Sources were consulted during the September 2026 release review. These sources support the specific word facts, not the game's effectiveness or every editorial difficulty choice.

## Adding or correcting a card

Keep a useful clue that does not reveal its target. Every build chip must have a known or word-specific explanation, and the chips must spell the target exactly. Add explicit spelling variants instead of inventing a catch-all letter-deletion rule. An inference needs at least three plausible choices and an authored answer index; the UI shuffles identities safely. Distinguish literal word sums from historical family questions. Hints should explain structure without including the target as a family example.

For a new base, provide both a build and inference route, or deliberately redesign and document the mastery model. Content tests check spelling, root resolution, route coverage and answer validity. A source reference and a clear correction are welcome contributions.
